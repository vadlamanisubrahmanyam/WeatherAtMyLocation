SkyPulse Weather is free with no ads and no accounts. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo (ECMWF, GFS, ICON, GEM, JMA and other national models). Radar is from RainViewer, satellite imagery from NASA GIBS, and the interactive map from Windy. Place search uses Open-Meteo Geocoding, and place names come from BigDataCloud and OpenStreetMap. US official alerts come from the National Weather Service. The app works out the derived alerts, activity scores and sun and moon times on your device.
